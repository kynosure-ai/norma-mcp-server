# DORA Third-Party ICT Risk Register (fixture)

NORMA MCP server local-development fixture.

## Purpose

Maintain an up-to-date register of all ICT third-party service arrangements,
including critical-or-important-function classification, exit strategies, and
sub-outsourcing chains, in compliance with DORA Articles 28 and 30.

## Scope

All ICT service contracts entered into by the financial entity.
