# NIS2 Incident Reporting Procedure (fixture)

NORMA MCP server local-development fixture (see iso27001-access-control.md
for context).

## Purpose

Define the workflow for detecting, classifying, and reporting significant
incidents to the national CSIRT within the NIS2 24-hour early warning,
72-hour notification, and one-month final report timeline.

## Scope

All operators of essential or important services subject to the NIS2 Directive.
